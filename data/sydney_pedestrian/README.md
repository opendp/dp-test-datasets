The Sydney Pedestrian Traffic data set logs the first sightings of an individual on Park Street, Bridge Street and Market Street, on February 3, 2024. 

The data is based on true counts of hourly per-street pedestrian traffic, but has additional synthetically-generated attributes: name, age and gender.
